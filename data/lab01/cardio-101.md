# CARDIO-101 Clinical Trial

## General Information

Trial ID: CARDIO-101

Phase: Phase III

Condition: Hypertension

## Inclusion Criteria

Participants must:

- Be between 40 and 70 years old.
- Have diagnosed hypertension.
- Have blood pressure greater than 140/90 mmHg.

## Exclusion Criteria

Participants must not:

- Have severe renal impairment.
- Be pregnant.
- Have participated in another clinical trial during the previous 30 days.

## Trial Sites

The trial is conducted at:

- Cairo Medical Center
- Alexandria Clinical Institute
