# ONCO-202 Clinical Trial

## General Information

Trial ID: ONCO-202

Phase: Phase II

Condition: Non-small-cell lung cancer

## Inclusion Criteria

Participants must:

- Be at least 18 years old.
- Have confirmed non-small-cell lung cancer.
- Have measurable disease.

## Exclusion Criteria

Participants must not:

- Have uncontrolled cardiovascular disease.
- Be pregnant.

## Trial Sites

The trial is conducted at:

- Cairo Oncology Center
- Alexandria Cancer Institute
